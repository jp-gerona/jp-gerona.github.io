## Add or refine evidence

When the author provides writing, ask whether he wants it used as a reusable voice reference if that intent is unclear. Approval of a factual correction, page design, or one draft for publication is not automatically approval as a general voice model.

For each explicitly approved sample, add a short entry here with:

- The exact approved excerpt, or a stable repository-relative link to it.
- Source and approval date, with the scope the author approved.
- Context and audience, such as a project note or personal introduction.
- What the author said works, preserving his feedback rather than inventing reasons.
- Any limits, such as dated factual claims or wording that applies only to that topic.

Store longer approved excerpts in this reference directory and link them here only when useful. Keep private messages and personal details out of the repository unless the author authorizes their inclusion. Prefer a concise excerpt he selects to an entire conversation dump.

If later feedback rejects a sample or narrows its use, remove it from active guidance or mark its scope explicitly. Do not silently blend contradictory examples into a personality profile. Use the sample closest to the current task while respecting the latest explicit preference.

Useful additional material would be a short introduction the author likes, rough notes explaining a real technical decision, and a paragraph he considers already finished. His comments on what sounds wrong are as useful as positive examples.
